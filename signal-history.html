Signal history

Questions

What counts as a zone change?

A recorded value that puts a signal in a different zone (BUY, neutral, near sell or SELL) than before. To filter out price jitter right at a level, the new zone has to hold for two recordings in a row, about 30 minutes, before it is logged. The same rule decides when alert emails are sent.

Why are only some signals listed?

Only signals that Signycle prices live are recorded every 15 minutes: Brent, WTI, the Brent–WTI spread, copper, aluminium, gold, steel, iron ore, lithium, the EUR 10-year rate and the US yield curve. Hand-updated signals change only when a new value is entered, so they have no continuous record.

Is there a backtest of past signals?

Not yet. A proper backtest needs a long, verified price history, and Signycle’s own recording only started in September 2026. The earlier backtest page used illustrative figures that had not been checked against market data, so it was removed. This log is the real record and grows every day.
