Multi-signal confirmation

A single indicator crossing a threshold is a warning. Multiple indicators confirming simultaneously is a high-conviction signal. In dec 2015, all three shipping signals fired within 6 weeks of each other.

Risk-adjusted returns

High returns mean nothing without context. A Sharpe ratio above 1.0 means you were well-compensated per unit of risk. Max drawdown shows the worst interim loss before final recovery.
