Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best NYSE cyclical stocks to buy in 2026?
Based on Signycle's macro signals, most NYSE commodity stocks are currently at late-cycle SELL levels — particularly energy (Brent ) and copper (FCX, SCCO with copper at /t). The best Signycle entry points come when signals are at BUY levels. Defence stocks (RTX, LMT, GD) are the exception — NATO rearmament is a structural multi-year trend that is not yet at a SELL level. How does Signycle track NYSE stocks?
Signycle uses 21 global macro signals — commodity prices, shipping rates, interest rates and economic indicators — to identify cycle turning points. These signals apply equally to NYSE and European stocks because the underlying commodities (copper, oil, steel) are global markets. Freeport-McMoRan (NYSE: FCX) and Boliden (Stockholm: BOL) both track LME copper. The signal is the same; only the stock exchange differs.
Is Freeport-McMoRan a good investment right now?
