Who built this

🌊
Signycle Team — Oslo, Norway
Founded 2025 · Bootstrapped · Independent

Signycle was built by a European investor with a background in financial markets and software development — frustrated by the lack of accessible cycle-aware tools for retail investors. The platform is independently operated from Oslo, with no outside investors, no conflicts of interest, and no financial relationship with any tracked stocks or commodities.

Transparency note: Signycle does not manage money, take investment positions or receive compensation from any financial services company. The founder holds personal investments in some of the cyclical sectors tracked on the platform — this is disclosed as a potential conflict of interest.

How this site is made

Signycle is built with heavy use of AI tools, and much of the writing on this site is AI-generated.

  • Signal values and zones are not written by AI. They are calculated by code from the sources listed on How it works — Yahoo Finance, the ECB and FRED for the live signals, and hand-entered values for the rest, each shown with the date it was last updated.
  • Some pages are reviewed by the founder before publishing; many are not. In particular, many of the research articles and company write-ups are AI-written and have not been individually reviewed.
  • That means mistakes are possible — an outdated figure, a wrong threshold or ticker. We fix errors as we find them, but please check any number before relying on it.

Spotted something wrong? Email hello@signycle.com — corrections are always welcome.

The problem we're solving

Cyclical stocks — energy, shipping, mining, agriculture — are among the most powerful wealth-building opportunities available to retail investors. A simple framework in 2020: buy Frontline when VLCC rates were below $15k/day, sell when they crossed $75k/day. Result: +621% in 20 months.

The problem is that nobody tells you when these signals cross thresholds. Energy investors watch Brent but don't know its historical SELL level. Shipping investors follow headlines but don't track BDI versus its 20-year cycle range. Mining investors read analyst reports but don't see copper's position in its long-run cycle.

Signycle fixes this. One platform. 24 signals. Each with a BUY zone, a SELL zone, and a current reading. Updated daily. Free.

Our philosophy

Data over narrative
Commodity cycles are driven by supply and demand economics, not stories. We show the data and let you decide. We do not make market calls.
History repeats
$100+ Brent has historically ended in demand destruction within 24 months — every single time. BDI below 900 has historically preceded 300–600% shipping stock rallies. These patterns are structural, not accidental.
Transparency
All data sources are publicly disclosed. All thresholds are explained with historical reasoning. We publish a dated log of every recorded zone change, including the ones that quickly reversed.
Accessible
The core platform is free. No paywall on signal data. No Bloomberg terminal required. Cyclical investing intelligence should be available to everyone.

Built in the open

Signycle launched in early 2026. As of March 2026 we have 3,020 unique visitors and coverage across 39 exchanges and 400+ stocks. We are in early access — the platform is live but we are actively improving it based on user feedback.

The platform is bootstrapped and independent. We are not venture-backed, we do not take advertising from financial services companies, and we have no financial relationship with any of the stocks or commodities we track. Revenue comes from the Early Access Pro subscription (in development).

We are based in Europe, with a focus on Nordic, European and global cyclical markets. Oslo Børs — the world's leading listing venue for shipping and offshore — is our home exchange.
