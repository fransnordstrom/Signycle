How it works

Cycles are predictable.
If you know what to watch.

New ships are ordered at cycle peaks and delivered into falling markets. Signycle identifies these patterns across every major cyclical sector and exchange — globally.

01
📡
We monitor the indicators
BDI, freight rates, order books, commodity prices, P/B ratios, PMI — updated daily across all tracked exchanges.
02
⚙️
The signal model evaluates
Historical thresholds determine whether each sector is in a buy, neutral, or sell zone — based on decades of cycle data.
03
📬
You get alerted
Email alerts only fire when enough signals align. No daily noise — just a message when history says it matters.
04
🧠
You decide
Signycle gives you the informational edge. The investment decision is always yours — we never give financial advice.
