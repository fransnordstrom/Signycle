Current Cycle Status — Switzerland

Global PMI 51.4 (NEUTRAL): Swiss industrials (ABB, Georg Fischer, Sika, Holcim) are mid-cycle. Switzerland's export-oriented industrial companies track global manufacturing and construction activity closely.

Gold $3,200+/oz (SELL): Gold at record highs is in SELL territory. Swiss gold-related stocks and funds have had exceptional runs — reduce exposure.

LME Copper (SELL): Copper at $12,043/t affects Swiss industrial companies as input cost and as a demand indicator for electrification projects (ABB, cables).

Sika (HOLD): Sika makes construction chemicals and is one of the strongest performers in a PMI-driven construction cycle. Mid-cycle at PMI 51.4 — hold.

Switzerland's industrial stocks are typically higher quality (more defensive margins, stronger balance sheets) than global peers — but this defensive quality means they also underperform in early-cycle bull markets.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best cyclical stocks on SIX Swiss Exchange?

ABB (ABBN.SW) for electrification and PMI, Sika (SIKA.SW) for construction chemicals, Holcim for cement, Georg Fischer for industrial PMI and Clariant for specialty chemicals are the strongest signal-driven cyclicals on Swiss Exchange.

How does global PMI affect Swiss industrials?

Switzerland exports precision machinery, chemicals and industrial equipment globally. ABB's robots, Georg Fischer's pipes and Sika's construction chemicals all depend on global manufacturing and construction activity tracked by PMI. Swiss companies tend to have more defensive margins — they don't boom as much in early cycles but hold up better in downturns.

Why is Barry Callebaut interesting for cyclical investors?

Barry Callebaut processes about a third of the world's cocoa into chocolate. Cocoa prices have surged to record highs in 2024 due to West African crop failures. This creates a margin squeeze for Barry Callebaut (who buys cocoa as input) but also revenue inflation. Watch cocoa price trends as the key signal.

How does gold at $3,200 affect Swiss Exchange?

Switzerland is the world's largest gold trading hub and many Swiss financial institutions, commodity traders and refiners are involved in gold. At $3,200+/oz, gold is in SELL territory. Swiss gold-related assets have had exceptional performance — the signal is to reduce, not add.
