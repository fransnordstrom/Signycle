How to use Signycle signals for sub-$2 stocks

The cyclical logic is identical for cheap stocks and expensive stocks — the signal drives the cycle, not the price. A stock trading at SGD 0.18 with a BDI buy signal has the same theoretical upside percentage as a stock at SGD 18 with the same signal.

AEM Holdings (AWX) — the SGX under-$2 case study

AEM Holdings was trading at SGD 0.85 in March 2020 with the WFE signal deep in buy territory. By August 2021 it had reached SGD 6.40 — a +890% return driven entirely by the semiconductor equipment upcycle. The signal was clear; the stock just happened to be cheap.

Signycle approach: Check the signal first — is BDI, Brent or EUR 10Y in buy zone? If yes, look at sub-$2 stocks in that sector. They typically have higher percentage upside than the large caps because institutional investors underweight them, creating larger mispricings at cycle troughs.

Pacific Basin Shipping (NC2) — the BDI play

Pacific Basin is Hong Kong's largest dry bulk operator but is well-followed by Singapore investors and heavily traded on SGX. At SGD 0.18, it is one of the purest BDI cycle plays available. When BDI rises from below 1,000 to above 2,500, Pacific Basin has historically delivered 300–400% returns. Current BDI at 2,095 is neutral — not yet a buy signal.

Golden Agri-Resources (E5H) — the Urea/Palm oil play

Golden Agri is one of the world's largest palm oil producers, with plantations primarily in Indonesia. At SGD 0.28, it offers exposure to the palm oil cycle at very low absolute cost. The Hormuz reopening today is a positive catalyst — urea prices should normalise, reducing Golden Agri's input costs for fertilisers on its plantations.

Risks of sub-$2 SGX stocks

  • Liquidity risk — many sub-$2 stocks have wide bid-ask spreads. For Pacific Basin and Golden Agri this is less of an issue, but for smaller names it can be costly
  • Rights issue risk — distressed companies often issue rights at a discount, diluting existing shareholders
  • Delisting risk — SGX has minimum trading price rules; stocks below SGD 0.20 for extended periods can face compliance issues
  • Signal timing — cheap stocks can stay cheap for years before the cycle turns. Position sizing and patience are critical
