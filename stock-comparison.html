Questions

What does this comparison show?

For each stock: its exchange, the signal Signycle links it to, that signal’s live value and zone, and how far the signal is from its BUY and SELL levels. It compares the cycles behind two companies, not their valuations, financials or share prices.

Why is there no verdict on which stock is better?

Because the signal zone alone can’t tell you that. Two companies on the same signal can have very different costs, debt and management. The comparison shows where each company’s main driver stands; the rest needs company research.

Can I share a comparison?

Yes. The address updates as you pick stocks, so you can copy the link from your browser.
